## Findings regarding the instruments of the Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR):

Universal Declaration on the Eradication of Hunger and Malnutrition
with URL:  https://www.ohchr.org/en/instruments-mechanisms/instruments/universal-declaration-eradication-hunger-and-malnutrition
It has no direct translation to any UNGA resolution. It is endorsed by 3348 (XXIX), which is the most close translation to a standard identifier such as General Assembly resolution. 
However, when a future document cite this Declaration, it is meant to cite the Declaration itself, its content; it is not straighforward to decide whether a future mention intends to refer the Declaration itself or the act of endorsement of the Declaration by the General Assembly.
PD: It does not even have a register in the Treaty Collection, we would need to do an identifier Ad hoc to refer this instrument.
